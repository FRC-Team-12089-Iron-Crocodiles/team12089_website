01 // ABOUT US

Who We Are As A Team

Team 12089, the Iron Crocs. We are a group of student engineers competing in the FIRST Robotics Competition. Operating out of our school, we design, test, and program competitive and other robots while maintaining a collaborative, hands-on engineering community.

02 // MISSION

Protecting the Everglades

We are a returning team whose mission is to raise awareness of the effect climate change is having on the Florida Everglades. Beyond building competitive robots, we use our platform to educate the community, advocate for local conservation, and implement sustainable practices in our engineering workflow. One example of our efforts is our robot Crunchy that remotely collects ground trash in environments that people may not be able to easily get to

03 // STRUCTURE

Divisions of Labor

To tackle the rigorous demands of the FRC, our team is split into sub teams that are more focused on their specific goals.

01 // HARDWARE

Build & Design

This team designs our robots. They make prototypes, keep designs up to date, oversees the work done to a robot, and maintains the tools we use.

02 // SOFTWARE

Programming

This team makes any software for the team (like this website). They program the robots, work with the design team for sensor placement, and help simulate how a robot would work before we build the final thing.

03 // OPERATIONS

Business & Outreach

This team helps spread our message and manage our money. They run our social media pages, research and apply for grants/sponorships, and are the most important part in our mission to protect the Everglades.
